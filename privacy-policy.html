Privacy Policy

Slerbo Enhancer browser extension
Last updated: 12 November 2026

Slerbo Enhancer is a browser extension that helps creators upload smoother, sharper videos on TikTok Studio. This policy explains exactly what information the extension uses, why, and what it never does. We keep it to the minimum needed for the tool to work.

What we collect

When you sign in with Telegram, our server stores only your public Telegram profile so we can recognize your account and show your plan:

  • Your Telegram numeric ID, first and last name, username, and profile photo URL.
  • Your plan, your daily upload count and your daily TikTok Checker count (so limits and Premium work).
  • The IDs of the TikTok videos you publish with the tool, so our server can check later that the public caption still carries the credit line (see "Caption credit").
  • Basic technical details of requests sent to our server (time, browser type / user-agent, and the extension's own fingerprint) — used only to keep the service secure (see "Security and misuse").

We do not collect your email, phone number, password, contacts, browsing history, or any TikTok account credentials.

Your videos stay on your device

  • Your video is processed on your own device and is never uploaded to us.
  • For the FPS Boost and Slerbo Pro modes, only the video's small technical header (timing metadata — not the picture or the sound) is sent to our server to prepare the file. It is used only for that request and is not stored.
  • Direct Upload sends nothing to us at all.

TikTok Checker

  • When you paste a TikTok link and press Check video, that link is sent to our server. The server reads the video's public information from TikTok (creator, caption, sound, engagement numbers, available qualities and visibility) and sends the result back to you.
  • The link and the result are not stored in your account. The server keeps the public video information in memory for up to 3 minutes so a repeated check is faster; only your daily check count is saved.
  • The Checker only reads information that TikTok shows publicly. It never signs in to TikTok and never accesses private data.
  • Country flags in the results are loaded as images from flagcdn.com; like any image, that site receives a normal request from your browser.

Permissions, and why

  • tiktok.com access: the extension runs only on tiktok.com — on TikTok Studio's upload page it adds its controls, prepares the selected video, shows upload progress and adds a short credit line to the caption; on other TikTok pages it only shows a small status badge.
  • Cookies: used only for the "Fix upload" button, which removes TikTok's own ttwid cookie to unstick a failed upload. We never read, collect, or send your cookies.
  • Storage: saves your settings and sign-in on your own device.
  • Clipboard (optional, asked only when you tap "Paste"): reads the clipboard once to paste a TikTok link into the Checker. Nothing from the clipboard is sent anywhere except that link, and only when you press Check video.
  • Our server (slerbo-auth.onrender.com): used for Telegram sign-in, checking your plan and daily limits, preparing the video for FPS Boost / Slerbo Pro, and the TikTok Checker.

Caption credit

Videos prepared with Slerbo Enhancer include a short credit line in the caption (ϟ Upload Method > @slerbo.edits and #slerbouploadmethod). This is part of how the tool works and is added when you post. After you publish, our server checks the video's public caption a few times over the following days to make sure the credit line is still there; removing it can lead to a warning or to losing access to the tool.

Security and misuse

To protect your account and the service, our server checks that requests really come from the official Slerbo Enhancer extension (its extension ID, its package fingerprint and a signature on Checker requests). Requests from modified copies of the extension, scripts or other apps are refused, and the Telegram account that sent them can be blocked. The technical details of such a request (time, browser type / user-agent, your Telegram ID and username) are shared only with the owner of Slerbo Enhancer, to review the case.

What we never do

  • We never sell or rent your data to anyone.
  • We never use your data for advertising or tracking across sites.
  • We never access your TikTok messages, followers, or account settings.
  • We never read your browsing on other websites — the extension runs only on tiktok.com.
  • We never share your data with third parties, except where required by law.

We only use the limited data above to run the tool you signed up for. Our use of this data follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your Telegram profile and plan are kept while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by contacting us on Telegram. For the credit check, only the IDs of your latest 30 published videos are kept. Removing the extension also clears everything it saved on your device.

Children

Slerbo Enhancer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. For any question about your data, or to request deletion, contact us on Telegram: @slerbo_freefire.

Slerbo Enhancer is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.
